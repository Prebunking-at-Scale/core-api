-- Background for O6 (the scraper's revisit rate). NOT a prerequisite for
-- simulate_d0_alert_levels.txt -- run that whenever you like; it reports its own cohort.
--
-- It cannot tell you how badly a past calc_date has eroded, so don't try to use it that
-- way. The erosion is "visited, unchanged, re-visited since" -- those videos carry
-- updated_at = the LATER visit and no video_stats row for the earlier one, so they are
-- invisible by construction. The last-visit date cannot count what it overwrote. This is
-- D0's whole point, showing up one level down.
--
-- What it DOES tell you -- half of O6, which the design doc calls the critical path:
--   * How fast the scraper sweeps: mass on today = fast, spread over days = slow.
--   * How much of the corpus is effectively dead (the long tail).
--   * Whether a large block sits at exactly today. If the pipeline runs at 00:05 and the
--     sweep clusters near midnight, that is the D0 day-boundary race, and it means
--     videos.updated_at is not good enough and we need the video_visits table.
--
-- The other half of O6 -- does the revisit strategy PRIORITISE active videos? -- is not
-- answerable here at all. That one is a question for whoever owns the scraper.

SELECT
    v.updated_at::date                                          AS last_visited_on,
    count(*)                                                    AS videos,
    round(100.0 * count(*) / sum(count(*)) OVER (), 2)          AS pct_of_all_videos,
    round(100.0 * sum(count(*)) OVER (ORDER BY v.updated_at::date DESC)
          / sum(count(*)) OVER (), 2)                           AS cumulative_pct
FROM videos v
GROUP BY v.updated_at::date
ORDER BY v.updated_at::date DESC
LIMIT 30;
